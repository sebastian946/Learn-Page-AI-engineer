## About this exam

This is the final, integrative exam of the course. Questions do not test one module at a time. Most are scenarios that cross boundaries: a retrieval change that affects evals, an agent tool that touches tenant isolation, a serving decision that moves cost and latency together. That is how real incidents and real interviews look.

**What it covers:**

- Multi-tenant isolation in vector stores, caches, agent tools and traces
- Retrieval quality (hybrid search, re-ranking, permission filters) and how to measure it
- Eval strategy: golden sets, CI gates, online sampling, trajectory evals
- Serving trade-offs: latency, cost, routing, caching, failure modes
- System design reasoning and capacity estimates
- Documenting and presenting your work

**How to prepare:** reread the mock system-design walkthrough (lesson 04) and the trade-off vocabulary (lesson 03). Skim your own capstone ADRs. For each question, ask "what would I measure to know?", because that is usually the correct instinct.

**Format:** 18 questions, 35 minutes. Single choice, multi-select, ordering and code-reading questions. Passing score is **70%**. You can retake it; options are shuffled on every attempt. Read each explanation after submitting, including the ones you got right.
