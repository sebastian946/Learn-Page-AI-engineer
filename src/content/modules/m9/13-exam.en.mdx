## About this exam

This exam checks that you can design and defend a decision-grade LLM evaluation, beyond the basics of Module 3. It has 18 questions and a 30-minute time limit. The passing score is **70%**.

**What it covers:**

- Eval design and task-specific metrics: per-class precision and recall, macro-F1, field-level F1, reference-based vs reference-free metrics
- Statistical rigor: Wilson and bootstrap intervals, paired comparisons, McNemar, sample size and power, non-inferiority, multiple comparisons
- Human evaluation: guidelines, Cohen's and Fleiss' kappa, Krippendorff's alpha, the kappa paradox, adjudication
- Advanced judges: reference-guided rubrics, G-Eval, juries, self-preference bias, prediction-powered inference, judge drift
- Frameworks: promptfoo, DeepEval custom metrics, Inspect AI, MLflow, and staying framework-agnostic
- Synthetic and metamorphic test data, multi-turn and agent evals, pass^k, production eval loops and benchmark literacy

**Question types:** single choice, multi-select, order-the-steps and code reading (statistics snippets, promptfoo YAML, a DeepEval custom metric).

**How to prepare:**

1. Recompute the worked examples in lessons 02 and 04 by hand: a Wilson interval, a kappa, a sample size.
2. Re-read your `01-stats/REPORT.md` and explain why the analysis is paired.
3. Be ready to say which unit you resample and why.
4. Review the "common mistakes" section of every lesson.

Options are shuffled on each attempt. Read every explanation after you submit.
