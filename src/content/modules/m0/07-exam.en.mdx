This exam checks that you can reason about the AI Engineer job market and your own plan, not that you memorized facts. It covers:

- The skill demand tiers: what is near-universal (RAG, prompt engineering, LLM integration, Python), what is very common (agents, evals, FastAPI, Docker/CI-CD, AWS, vector databases) and what is secondary (fine-tuning, data platforms).
- The three role families: AI-first, AI-support and ML Engineer, and where a senior SDET fits best.
- How your QA skills map to AI engineering: golden datasets, thresholds, CI gates, non-determinism.
- Reading a job posting: the four-pass method, must-haves vs nice-to-haves, red flags.
- What a strong portfolio README contains and what a reviewer checks in two minutes.
- How the platform, the exam gate, spaced review and the interview bank work.

There are 14 questions: single choice, multi-select, ordering and short code-reading. You have 25 minutes. The passing score is **70%**, and passing unlocks Module 1.

**How to prepare:** re-read the "Common mistakes" sections of each lesson, and make sure your `ai-engineer-portfolio` repo is pushed with the README linter green. Several questions describe situations rather than definitions. If you fail, read every explanation, review the related lesson and retake. Questions and options are shuffled each attempt.
